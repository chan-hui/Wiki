# Achievements
Achievements those require winning a match can only be acquired in ranked matches.
Achievements can be found on your ranked profile and up to 3-5 achievements of your choosing can be highlighted. highlighted achievements will also be seen by your opponent when entering a match.
You can highlight up to 3 achievement by default, up to 4 achievements with supporter tier 2, and up to 5 achievements with supporter tier 3. [link to the supporter page]

Some achievements can be leveled up, or obtained by doing something once then winning (see list below)
